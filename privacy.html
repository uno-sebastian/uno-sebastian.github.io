Privacy Policy

Home Automation App

Effective November 17, 2026

Who runs this app

This is a private home automation app run by Sebastian Echeverry (“I”, “me”) for my own household. It is not a commercial service and is not open to the public. Only people I have personally invited can sign in.

What information the app collects

The app uses Sign in with Google to confirm who is signing in. When you sign in, Google shares:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your profile picture
  • a unique Google account ID

The app does not ask for your Google password, and it does not access your Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google data.

While you use the app, it may also keep basic technical records, such as sign-in times and which home devices or automations you used, so the app works and I can fix problems.

How the information is used

  • To check that you are someone allowed to use the app
  • To keep you signed in and show your name in the app
  • To run, secure and troubleshoot the app

Your information is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Sharing

I do not share your information with anyone, except when the law requires it. The app runs on a server I control; Google handles sign-in under its own privacy policy.

Google user data

This app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Your information is stored on a server I run, accessed over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and limited to what the app needs. I keep it only while you have access to the app.

Your choices

  • You can remove the app’s access at any time from your Google Account connections page.
  • You can ask me to see, correct or delete your information, and I will delete it within 30 days.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the effective date above.
