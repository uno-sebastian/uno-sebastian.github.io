Home Automation App

A private app by Sebastian Echeverry

What it is

A private home automation app I run for my own household. It lets invited members of my household see and control our home devices and automations from one place.

Signing in

The app uses Sign in with Google only to confirm who you are. It reads your name, email address and profile picture, and nothing else from your Google account. Only people I have invited can get in.
